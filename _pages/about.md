  <h1>Tingwei Chen</h1>
  <p>I am a PhD student at the University of Technology Sydney, supervised by Prof. Min Xu.
    My research brings together artificial intelligence and wireless perception, with interests
    in mmWave sensing, Wi-Fi sensing, and large language model applications.</p>
  <p>I received my master’s degree in Communications Engineering from The Chinese University
    of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (2021–2023), and was a Visiting Student at the Shenzhen Research
    Institute of Big Data (2023–2025). I also worked as a Research Intern at Huawei 2012 Labs,
    contributing to Wi-Fi 8 protocol pre-research.</p>
  <p>Outside of research, I enjoy playing basketball and volleyball.</p>
