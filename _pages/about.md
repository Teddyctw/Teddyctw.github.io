  <p class="eyebrow">ABOUT</p>
  <h1>Tingwei Chen</h1>
  <p>I am a PhD student at the University of Technology Sydney, supervised by Prof. Min Xu.
    My current research interests center on AI-powered mmWave radar for environment perception,
    human sensing, and embodied perception.</p>
  <p>I aim to explore radar-based scene understanding and mapping, human motion and activity
    understanding, and the fusion of radar, vision, and language for embodied agents.
    My broader goal is to connect perception of environments and people with navigation
    and interaction in the physical world, building on my background in wireless sensing
    and multimodal AI.</p>
  <p>I received my master’s degree in Communications Engineering from The Chinese University
    of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (2021–2023), and was a Visiting Student at the Shenzhen Research
    Institute of Big Data (2023–2025). I also worked as a Research Intern at Huawei 2012 Labs,
    contributing to Wi-Fi 8 protocol pre-research.</p>
  <p>Outside of research, I enjoy playing basketball and volleyball.</p>
