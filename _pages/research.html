Research directions I am pursuing: sensing environments and people with mmWave radar, towards embodied intelligence.

Radar-based environment perception

I aim to explore how mmWave radar can capture spatial structure, objects, and scene dynamics, moving from radar measurements to representations that support mapping and semantic understanding of the surrounding environment.

Spatial understanding / Scene representation / Radar mapping

Human sensing & motion understanding

I am interested in learning human presence, motion, pose, and activities from mmWave radar, and understanding how people interact with their surroundings. This direction builds on my earlier work in Wi-Fi sensing and fall detection.

Human motion / Pose estimation / Activity recognition

Embodied & multimodal perception

I aim to investigate how radar, vision, and language can work together to give embodied agents an understanding of environments and human behavior, and how these perceptual representations can support navigation and interaction.

Radar–vision fusion / Embodied intelligence / Perception for action
