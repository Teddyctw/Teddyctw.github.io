MMWAVE RADAR & EMBODIED PERCEPTION

PhD Student · University of Technology Sydney

I am interested in AI-powered mmWave radar perception, with a focus on environment perception, human sensing, and embodied perception. My goal is to help intelligent systems understand their surroundings, perceive people, and act in the physical world.

I am a PhD student at UTS, supervised by Prof. Min Xu. Building on my work in wireless sensing and multimodal AI, I aim to connect radar-based perception with scene understanding, human motion understanding, and embodied intelligence.
